# Updating the Global Insights Collective website

Send requests from **iwarsame8@gmail.com** to **updates@globalinsightscollective.com**. This is the only approved sender, including replies that approve publication. No website login is needed.

Use a subject such as “Website update — consulting service”. Include:

- The page or link to update.
- What to add, change or remove, with the exact facts and wording where important.
- Relevant images, text or a PDF. Attach the actual material instead of only linking to it.
- A specific date and time if the content is time-sensitive. A deadline is a request, not a guaranteed publication time.

Keep one topic in each email conversation. Reply in the same conversation for corrections and answers to questions. Plain-text emails are the most reliable. Keep attachments below 5 MB each and the whole email below 10 MB.

The assistant acknowledges your request, checks whether it fits the existing website, and prepares a preview. It may ask for missing facts or suggest an alternative if your request conflicts with the site's design. It will not invent credentials, affiliations or service claims.

Open the preview and check the wording, images, links and layout. Reply **Publish** to that latest preview email only when you want that version made public. To change it, reply with your corrections instead. An old preview approval cannot publish a newer version. You receive a completion email after the live site is checked. Silence never approves publication.

Reply **Cancel** to stop the request. Reply **Undo** in the same conversation to request reversal of its published change; if later changes make that unsafe, it goes for operator review.

Unresolved problems go through a stronger agent and then to the operator. Large redesigns, forms, navigation, accounts and new functionality require operator handling.

You may receive one reminder after 48 hours and a monthly check-in after a quiet month. Reply **Unsubscribe** to stop reminders and check-ins. You can still request updates.

Preview links are public to anyone who has the link. Do not send confidential material intended to stay private. The assistant is not guaranteed to catch every factual or visual problem: your preview review is the final approval.
